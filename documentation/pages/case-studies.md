# Case Studies

The **Case Studies** library contains pre-built, guided investigations that demonstrate Iris's analytical capabilities using real Earth observation data. Each case study walks through a complete evidence trail for a known or historically significant environmental trend.

> Navigate to it via: **Workspace → Case Studies** in the left sidebar, or browse the [Case Studies page](/case-studies) on the main website.

---

## What case studies include

Each case study is a fully loaded investigation that includes:

- **Context** — a plain-language description of the environmental issue being investigated
- **Dataset and region** — the specific variable, provider, time window, and geographic area
- **Full Case File** — a complete statistical analysis with trend charts, Mann-Kendall results, Sen's slope, and anomaly detection
- **Interpretation** — an explanation of what the numbers mean in environmental terms
- **Related research** — links to relevant peer-reviewed literature and NASA technical documentation
- **Next clues** — suggested follow-on investigations using related variables

## Example case studies

### Two decades of urban warming — Los Angeles, USA
**Variable:** Near-surface air temperature | **Period:** 2000–2024 | **Provider:** NASA POWER / MERRA-2

Los Angeles has experienced consistent surface warming over the past two decades, amplified by the urban heat island effect. This case study detects a statistically significant warming trend of **+0.42°C per decade** (\(p = 0.0014\), Hamed–Rao Modified Mann–Kendall; 95% slope CI: [+0.22, +0.63] °C/decade), isolates the urban warming signal from regional climate variability, and identifies anomalously hot summers in 2020 and 2023.

---

### The signature of a historic drought — Central Valley, USA
**Variable:** Total precipitation | **Period:** 1990–2022 | **Provider:** NASA POWER / GPCP

California's Central Valley experienced one of the most severe multi-year droughts on record from 2012 to 2017. This case study investigates the long-term precipitation trend, identifies the drought signature in the deseasonalized record, and compares it to the concurrent soil moisture and vegetation stress signals.

---

### Forest resilience under pressure — Amazon Basin, Brazil
**Variable:** Vegetation index (NDVI) | **Period:** 2000–2024 | **Provider:** MODIS (MOD13C2)

The Amazon Basin shows a statistically significant decline in vegetation health over 24 years, with accelerating browning in the southeastern transition zone. This case study applies NDVI trend analysis to characterize the spatial pattern of forest stress and identifies potential links to precipitation deficits.

## Loading a case study

From the Case Studies page:

1. Browse or search the library of available investigations
2. Click **"Load investigation"** on any case to open it in the workspace
3. The investigation pre-loads the dataset, region, extraction result, and Case File
4. All data shown is real — Iris never uses fabricated numbers in case studies

---

## Checkpoint-based case studies (Guided Investigation Format)

Some case studies are structured as **checkpointed walkthroughs** — step-by-step guided investigations where you answer questions and make choices at each stage. These are designed for educational use and rigorous scientific demonstrations.

Checkpoint studies guide you through:
1. Forming the investigation question
2. Selecting and validating data
3. Interpreting the statistical output
4. Drawing evidence-based conclusions
